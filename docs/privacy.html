Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · App: Islamic Habits · Package: dev.smoothchocolate.islamic_habits

The short version. Islamic Habits does not have accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. Your habits, prayer settings, and tasbeeh counts stay on your device. The app does not send them to the developer or to anyone else.

What the app stores on your device

The app stores the following locally, using Android's app-private storage:

  • Habits, their schedules, colors, icons, and completion history
  • Prayer-time settings, including the latitude and longitude you choose
  • Prayer reminder and Adhan preferences
  • Tasbeeh presets, goals, counts, and appearance settings
  • App preferences such as language, theme, start of week, and widget transparency

This data is not transmitted to the developer. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Network access

The app requests the INTERNET permission for one purpose: loading map tiles while you choose a location with the map picker. Those tiles come from the OpenStreetMap Foundation's public tile service at tile.openstreetmap.org.

When map tiles load, the tile server receives standard web-request information such as your IP address, the requested tile coordinates, and your app's user agent. This is necessary to deliver the map. The OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy applies to that request: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy.

The app does not use the internet for accounts, sync, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or push notifications.

Notifications

Prayer reminders, Adhan notifications, and habit reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent through a push-notification server, and no notification content leaves your device.

Backups

Backup & Restore creates a file only when you ask it to. You choose where that file goes (for example, a cloud drive or email). The app does not upload backups itself.

Permissions and why they are used

PermissionPurpose
NotificationsPrayer, Adhan, and habit reminders
Exact alarmsFire reminders at the intended minute
Receive boot completedRestore scheduled reminders after a reboot
Ignore battery optimizations (optional)Keep time-sensitive reminders reliable on aggressive battery managers
InternetLoad OpenStreetMap tiles in the location picker

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or advertising-ID permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because the app does not collect data at all, there is nothing to delete on a server.

Your choices and data deletion

  • Uninstall the app or use Android's Clear storage to delete all app data from the device.
  • Delete any backup files you exported, wherever you saved them.
  • Turn off notifications or individual reminder types in the app or in Android settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the new version will be published at the same URL. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
