## Notes for review

- The app contains Qur'anic/dhikr text and prayer guidance. This is devotional content, not proselytizing or user-generated content.
- There are no social features, comments, profiles, or messaging.
- There are no external links except the OpenStreetMap tile requests, which are not user-navigable content.
- The app is free, with no ads and no purchases.
