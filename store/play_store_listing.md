## Full description (English)

```text
Build a steady daily rhythm around prayer, Qur'an, and the habits that matter to you — without an account, ads, or tracking.

Islamic Habits combines a prayer-times companion, a habit tracker, and a digital tasbeeh in one calm, private app.

PRAYER TIMES
• Accurate times calculated on your device from your chosen location
• City search, manual coordinates, or pick a point on the map
• Multiple calculation methods and madhabs
• Per-prayer manual adjustments
• Hijri and Gregorian dates side by side

ADHAN AND REMINDERS
• 8 bundled Adhan reciters, each with a full recording and a shorter Takbeer-only version
• Choose per prayer: full notification, Adhan audio, silent vibration, or nothing
• Optional reminder 30 minutes after each prayer, answerable directly from the notification
• Habit reminders with daily, weekday, or times-per-week schedules

HABIT TRACKING
• Yes/No habits such as "prayed Fajr" or number habits such as pages of Qur'an read
• Daily, selected-weekday, or N-times-per-week schedules
• Custom colors, icons, success targets, and streaks
• A one-tap home screen for today's habits
• Archive habits you want to pause without losing their history

TASBEEH COUNTER
• Swipe-to-count pearl with a peaceful full-screen design
• After-prayer, general, salawat, istighfar, and other dhikr presets
• Goals of 33, 100, 1000, or unlimited
• Bead colors and geometric patterns that unlock with your longest habit streak

PROGRESS THAT MOTIVATES
• Overall success rate and best streak
• Daily, weekly, and monthly completion charts
• Per-habit breakdown of completed, missed, and unlogged days
• Full right-to-left chart support for Arabic, Persian, and Urdu

HOME SCREEN WIDGET
• Today's prayer times at a glance
• Live countdown to the next prayer
• Automatically moves to the next prayer without opening the app
• Adjustable transparency

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account and no sign-up
• No ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs
• Habit and prayer data is stored only on your device
• Optional backup file that you export and keep wherever you choose
• Internet is used only to load map tiles while you pick a location

MADE FOR EVERYDAY USE
• Dark mode
• Configurable start of week
• 9 languages: English, Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Indonesian, Japanese, Norwegian, Swedish, and Finnish
• Right-to-left layouts and optional Eastern Arabic numerals
```

## Localized listings

The app itself is translated into 9 languages, but Play listings are separate per language. Prioritize these translations before launch:

1. Arabic (`ar`)
2. Urdu (`ur`)
3. Persian (`fa`)
4. Indonesian (`id`)

The English listing can be reused as the fallback for the remaining locales.
